**TTS default voice fix (PR #35) — open, awaiting review, not yet merged.** Real-device testing found Scout speaking with an unwanted female voice on Diana's Galaxy S24 (defaults to Samsung TTS) while the Fold 7 and A32 (both default to Google TTS) sound correct — a real first-use problem, not theoretical: Diana stopped testing Scout over it. Scout had never explicitly chosen a voice before this — only `tts.language = Locale.US`, inheriting whatever the bound engine defaulted to. Investigated via a standalone, read-only diagnostic tool (outside this repo, never touching Scout or device TTS settings) to inspect real voice data on all three phones; `en-us-x-iom-local` was verified by listening on real Fold 7/A32 hardware, never inferred from its name. ## 3. Scout's Core Philosophy

Scout should feel: Calm. Thoughtful. Quietly alive. Emotionally subtle. Occasionally curious.
Scout should NOT feel: Excited. Scripted. Fake. Cartoonish. Hyperactive. Constantly praising.

**Stability > Features | Presence > Intelligence | Honest > Fake cheerful | Local-first > Cloud | Predictable > Flashy**

Scout must never fake a signal he cannot actually observe — no invented emotion, laughter detection, or "focus" reading. This is a hard, explicit design rule, most recently reaffirmed for Companion Moments (see above).
